Quick tutorial while the kiosk is idle

1) Select Profile

Tap your user tile to enter your profile and load your saved progress.

2) Build Workout

Choose a muscle group or use Build a Workout for single-day and weekly plans.

3) Add Nutrition

Open Training Nutrition & Fuel Guide to pair meals with training goals.

4) Share to Phone

Use QR share for workouts, meals, favorites, calendar, and private stats (login required).
