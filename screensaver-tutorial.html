    After Login: Choose Your Starting Point
    🧭

    Your personalized start menu

    Choose one of three options:
    • Continue on your own opens the regular main menu.
    • What would you like to do today? offers Work out, Stretch, Nutrition plan, Challenge another user, and Create a personal workout plan.
    • Guided tour explains each panel and how QR sharing works.
    • In Work out, choose Muscle Groups or Full Body Workout. Use Back to return to the previous choices.

    💡 Tip: Sign in with any profile to keep your progress and personal records with that user.

    Work Out, Then Update Your Progress
    💪

    Exercises, sets and personal records

    Choose your workout path:
    • Muscle Groups opens exercise cards by body area; Full Body Workout creates a balanced exercise list.
    • Create a personal workout plan opens the Interactive Coach.
    • Read the exercise instructions, then tap Track Your Progress.
    • Enter each set's weight and reps, choose the date completed, then save.
    • Your exercise history and best estimated lift appear in My Personal Records.

    📊 Result: Progress is attached to your profile and dated for the day you completed the exercise.

    More Ways to Train
    🥗

    Training support tools

    Choose the panel that fits your day:
    • Stretch opens body-area mobility routines.
    • Nutrition plan builds a single-day or weekly Training Nutrition & Fuel Guide.
    • Challenge another user opens the daily challenge and friend challenge panel.
    • Personal workout plan opens the Interactive Coach.

    🎯 Tip: Choose Guided tour after login for a panel-by-panel walkthrough.

    Scan Your Workout to Your Phone
    📱

    QR Code Sharing

    Take your selected workout with you:
    • On the kiosk, open a workout or exercise list and tap Send your workout to your phone by scanning the QR code with your camera.
    • Point your phone camera at the kiosk QR code and tap the link.
    • The phone page shows the exercise how-to instructions and workout details.
    • Enter weight, reps, and completion date on each exercise; add another entry for each set.
    • Sign in on the phone and tap Save Progress to sync logs and PRs to your account.

    📲 Privacy: Phone logs stay on the phone until you sign in and sync. Only your account can retrieve synced exercise logs.
